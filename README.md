# StayPK Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

## Introduction

StayPK ("we", "our", or "us") is a platform that connects guests with property hosts across Pakistan.

By using StayPK, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

## Information We Collect

We may collect:

- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Profile photo
- Booking information
- Property information
- Device information
- Push notification token
- Payment screenshots submitted for booking verification

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Create your account
- Process bookings
- Connect guests and hosts
- Send notifications
- Verify payments
- Improve our services
- Prevent fraud

## Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Your information may only be shared with:

- Property hosts
- Guests involved in a booking
- Our support team
- Service providers required to operate the app

## Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

## Children's Privacy

StayPK is not intended for users under 18 years of age.
