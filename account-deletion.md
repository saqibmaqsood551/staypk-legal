# StayPK Account Deletion

Last updated: July 2026

## Requesting Account Deletion

If you would like to permanently delete your StayPK account and associated personal information, please send an email to:

**support@staypk.app**

Subject:
```
Account Deletion Request
```

Please include:
- Your registered email address
- Your full name
- Your phone number (if available)

This helps us verify your identity before deleting your account.

---

## What Data Will Be Deleted

After verification, we will permanently delete:

- Your account
- Your profile information
- Your profile photo
- Your saved properties (if you are a host)
- Your booking history (where legally permitted)
- Your payment information submitted through the app
- Your messages with our support team
- Push notification tokens

---

## Data We May Retain

Some information may be retained when required by applicable laws or for fraud prevention, dispute resolution, taxation, or legal compliance.

Any retained information is securely stored and is not used for marketing purposes.

---

## Processing Time

Account deletion requests are normally completed within **7 business days**.
